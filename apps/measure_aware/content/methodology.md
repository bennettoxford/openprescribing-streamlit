We have based our measure on the [England-adapted 2024 AWaRe categories](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/uk-aware-antibiotic-classification/uk-access-watch-reserve-and-other-classification-for-antibiotics-uk-aware-antibiotic-classification#list-of-uk-aware-antibiotic-classifications) published by the [UK Health Security Agency](https://www.gov.uk/government/organisations/uk-health-security-agency).  Although this generally involved mapping to chemical substances, due to differences between some routes for the same drug (e.g. fosfomyin used orally is *Access*, whereas used intravenously it is considered *Reserve*), we have also had to map to dm+d form routes as well, to ensure accuracy.

We then group the total number of prescription items for eaach AWaRE category to get the proportions for all AWaRE categories for all organisations.
